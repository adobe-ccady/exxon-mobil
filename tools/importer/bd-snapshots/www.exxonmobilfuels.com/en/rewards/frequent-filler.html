Frequent Fillers: Get extra value when you fuel regularly

Join the Exxon Mobil Rewards+™ program

Start earning even more points every month to save on fuel, snacks, car washes and more — that's real savings.
Join Rewards
Sign in
Exxon Mobil Rewards+ members who fill up with at least 100 gallons of fuel every month will earn 1¢ extra in points per gallon on fuel purchases and 2¢ extra in points per dollar spent on eligible purchases inside the convenience store every day. In addition, first-time qualifiers will receive a one-time welcome bonus of 100 points. And on the 28th of every month, Frequent Filler Member day, members will earn $1 in extra bonus points on a fuel purchase of 8 gallons or more.

  • What’s Frequent Filler?

    Earn extra points when you purchase 100 gallons of fuel or more in a calendar month with the Exxon Mobil Rewards+ program.

  • What do I get?

    Get 1¢ in extra points per gallon of fuel and 2¢ in extra points per dollar on select in-store purchases for the rest of the month and for the following month.

  • Keep it going

    Reach 100 gallons again next month to extend your bonus another month.

Frequently asked questions

Get answers to questions on Exxon Mobil Rewards+ benefits and the Frequent Filler program.
How do I earn points with the Exxon Mobil Rewards+ program?
With the Exxon Mobil Rewards+ program, you can earn points on items you purchase every day — like fuel, convenience store items and car washes. You can redeem every 100 points you earn for $1 in value. You will earn 3 points per gallon on fuel and 2 points for every $1 you spend on select purchases in the convenience store or on car washes.
What is the Exxon Mobil Rewards+ Frequent Filler program?

Frequent Filler is a bonus program within the Exxon Mobil Rewards+ program. To receive Exxon Mobil Rewards+ Frequent Filler bonus points, you must be a registered member of Exxon Mobil Rewards+ and purchase 100 or more gallons of fuel in a calendar month (e.g., August).

Once you qualify as a Frequent Filler, you will earn 1 extra point on fuel and 2 extra points on convenience store purchases on qualifying purchases of fuel and eligible convenience store items at Exxon™ and Mobil™ branded stations. You will receive the benefit for the remainder of the month plus one month (e.g., qualify in August and receive the benefit through September). Continue to purchase 100 gallons or more every month to maintain your Frequent Filler bonus points.

Legal disclaimers

The Exxon Mobil Rewards+ program

Exxon Mobil Rewards+™ is a rewards program available at participating Exxon™ and Mobil™ branded service stations. You must fully enroll to become a member and use points. Go to Exxon Mobil Rewards+ website or app to complete enrollment. Points have no monetary value and expire on December 31 of each year. Any Points that are at least one (1) year old will expire. The age of a Point will be measured from the date the point is issued on the transaction. Members are eligible to earn points for fuel and in-store savings.

We reserve the right to terminate your Exxon Mobil Rewards+ account at any time, or to modify or restrict your ability to use points, immediately and without notice. We may modify these terms in any manner, at any time, in our sole discretion. Terms and conditions apply. View full Exxon Mobil Rewards+ program terms and conditions.

Exxon Mobil Rewards+ Frequent Filler program

Exxon Mobil Rewards+ Frequent Filler status is obtained by purchasing at least 100 gallons of Supreme+ premium fuel in a calendar month (e.g., August). To qualify, you must make purchases at participating Exxon™ or Mobil™ branded stations either (1) within the Exxon Mobil Rewards+ app, or using (2) your phone number (Alt-ID) or (3) your Exxon Mobil Rewards+ card.

Exxon Mobil Rewards+ Frequent Filler benefits (“benefits”) begin after Frequent Filler status is earned. You must purchase at least 100 gallons each consecutive calendar month to maintain your benefits. In the event that your Frequent Filler requirement is not maintained in the following month, your Frequent Filler status and benefits will expire.

Benefits include earning an extra point on all fuel purchases and 2 extra points on all convenience store purchases after earning and while maintaining Frequent Filler status. You will also be eligible for various Frequent Filler offers and experiences that are available to Frequent Filler members.

Your Frequent Filler benefits are non-transferable and do not preclude you from using other Exxon Mobil Rewards+ offers, unless specified. Terms and conditions apply.
